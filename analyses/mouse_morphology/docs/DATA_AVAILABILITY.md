# Data availability and redistribution boundary

The mouse Patch-seq measurements and morphology reconstructions were generated
in the present study. No public GEO, SRA, DANDI, NeMO or equivalent accession
was identified in the frozen archive on 2026-09-30. The permanent accession must
be inserted after deposition; macaque HMBA/NeMO/DANDI identifiers must not be
assigned to this mouse module.

This repository includes analysis-ready derived tables, frozen cell labels,
statistics, figure source data, layouts and publication figures needed to audit
the reported conclusions. Original ASC reconstructions are not embedded because
their redistribution status and mounted source were unavailable during archive
assembly. Their expected filenames and cell mappings are retained in
`audit/RAW_ASC_acquisition_status_for_final187.csv`.

Complete high-volume run tables and 600-dpi TIFFs are staged outside Git in the
local release-assets directory. Their filenames, byte sizes, SHA-256 values and
planned upload location are recorded in `audit/release_assets_manifest.csv`.
They were not uploaded as a Release in this task.

Frozen Mouse MSN morphology M1-M4: Macaque-M-matched 500-repeat validation
=====================================================================================
Scope: 187 frozen morphology cells (M1=67, M2=23, M3=62, M4=35) from 105 recording-day proxy groups.

Leakage control: minimum shifting, column-sum normalization, log1p transformation, Z-scoring and three-component PCA were re-estimated in every training or resampling set; recording-day groups never crossed training and test partitions. Frozen workflow reproduction: ARI=1.000, NMI=1.000.

Evidence boundary: supervised results measure recoverability of frozen labels from the same morphology domain; unsupervised resampling measures de novo four-class recovery; cell margins quantify assignment stability. Animal IDs are unavailable and recording day is only a conservative animal proxy, so this is internal repeated validation rather than independent-cohort validation.
